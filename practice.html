It starts gently, with vectors by hand, and climbs one lab at a time towards the four artifacts. Read nothing first. How this works

Drills are the functions of the labs' milestones, in a ramp: a warm-up through A0's first four labs, then each artifact's labs with the labs they build on slipped in first. The editor opens at your function; write it, then press Check; Python runs in your browser, nothing is installed. Your code, hints shown and drills passed are saved in this browser (back them up from the Review page). When every drill of a lab passes, the lab is marked complete in its course. Select any sentence and press Explain to ask about it.
