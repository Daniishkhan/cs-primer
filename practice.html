How this works

Drills are the milestones of the labs, in the order the four artifacts need them. Press Edit to write your function in the file, then Check; Python runs in your browser, nothing is installed. Your code, hints shown and drills passed are saved in this browser (back them up from the Review page). When every drill of a lab passes, the lab is marked complete in its course. Select any sentence and press Explain to ask about it.
