Near-duplicate pages share most of their text but not all of it, so we score the overlap. A shingle is a run of n consecutive words; the similarity of two texts is the number of shingles they share divided by the number of distinct shingles in either.

shingles(text, n) returns the set of n-word windows of the lower-cased, whitespace-split text, and jaccard(a, b) is the similarity of two such sets. A signature compresses a set: each of the first num_perm pairs in HASH_COEFS is one hash function, applied to the base_hash of each shingle, and minhash_signature keeps each function's smallest value. For one function, the two minima agree exactly when the smallest hash over the union belongs to both sets, which has probability equal to the similarity. So estimate_jaccard is the fraction of positions where two signatures agree.
