A deduplicator can miss real duplicates or merge pages that are merely similar; precision (the share of reported pairs that are real) and recall (the share of real pairs reported) count the two mistakes. Reported pairs then form clusters, groups linked by any chain of pairs, and one page survives per cluster.

precision_recall(predicted, gold) compares two sets of pairs. clusters(ids, pairs) finds the connected groups: pairs chain, whichever way round each is written, so a union-find or a graph search does it. near_dedupe chains the given helpers (shingles, minhash_signature, candidate_pairs, jaccard), drops candidates whose true similarity is below min_jaccard and keeps the smallest id of each cluster. run_pipeline calls the stages in the docstring's order and records every drop with its stage, so each page is either kept or dropped, exactly once.

clusters(["a","b","c","d"], [("a","b"), ("c","b")]) = [["a","b","c"], ["d"]]
