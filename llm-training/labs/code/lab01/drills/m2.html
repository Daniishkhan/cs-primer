Cheap rules can discard junk before any model reads it. Some judge one line (does it look like a real sentence?), others judge a whole document by simple measurements.

c4_line_filter tests each line against the constants MIN_LINE_WORDS, BAD_LINE_SUBSTRINGS and TERMINAL. quality_flags makes these measurements on the whole text and returns the names of the rules broken, in the docstring's order: word count, mean word length, # and ... per word, share of lines starting with a bullet or ending in ..., share of words containing a letter, distinct stop words present (lower-cased, punctuation stripped), share of repeated lines, and "lorem ipsum" or { anywhere. The thresholds are constants in the file. Guard every division so the empty text is flagged length instead of crashing. keep is true when no flag fires.
