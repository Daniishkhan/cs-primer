Cheap rules can discard junk before any model reads it. Some judge one line (does it look like a real sentence?), others judge a whole document by simple measurements.

c4_line_filter tests each line against the constants MIN_LINE_WORDS, BAD_LINE_SUBSTRINGS and TERMINAL. quality_flags measures the whole text and returns the names of the rules it breaks, in the docstring's order. In that order the measurements are: word count; mean word length; # and ... per word; share of bullet lines; share of lines ending in ...; share of words with a letter; distinct stop words such as the, of and that (lower-cased, punctuation stripped); share of repeated lines; "lorem ipsum" or {. The thresholds are constants in the file. Guard every division so the empty text is flagged length instead of crashing; keep is true when no flag fires.
