For a group of answers to the same question, the useful signal is which ones beat the rest of the group. An answer's advantage is its reward compared with the group's own typical reward, so no second model is needed to judge them.

group_advantages has three modes for a group of G rewards. "std" also divides by the population standard deviation plus eps (so a tied group does not divide by zero), which boosts rare outcomes; "mean" only subtracts the group mean; "rloo" subtracts the mean of the other rewards. The group mean includes the answer itself, so it biases the gradient slightly, and leave-one-out does not. In every mode a group whose rewards all tie must give exact zeros, and zero_signal reports it.
