Each sampled answer has a ratio: how much likelier the policy finds it now than a frozen copy did when the group was drawn. A clip caps how much that movement is rewarded, and a penalty, estimated from the one sample, charges for drifting away from the reference.

ratio works through log-probabilities. clipped_objective is the smaller of rho * A and the same product with rho forced into [1 - eps, 1 + eps] (A is the advantage), and it is a quantity to maximise. k3 is r - log r - 1 with r = pi_ref(a) / pi_theta(a), never negative. grpo_loss_and_grad returns the loss and its gradient on the logits. In the docstring both gradient terms share the factor (e_a - p), the slope of the sampled answer's log-probability; only the policy term vanishes while the clip is active.

pi_ref(a) = 0.5, pi_theta(a) = 1: r = 0.5, k3 = 0.5 - ln 0.5 - 1 = 0.1931
eps 0.2, A = +1, rho = 1.5: objective min(1.5, 1.2) = 1.2, policy gradient 0
