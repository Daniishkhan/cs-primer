Training repeats one cycle: pick some questions, freeze a copy of the policy, sample a group of answers per question from the copy and score them with the verifier. Then take several gradient steps on those same groups; because every step is measured against the frozen copy and clipped, the policy cannot run far on samples it has already used.

train_grpo starts from a copy of task.ref and follows the docstring's order of random draws. Per step, pick the questions with rng.sample; for each, copy the current logits as z_old, draw the group with sample_index from softmax(z_old), get rewards from the verifier and advantages from group_advantages. Then repeat inner times: for every group call grpo_loss_and_grad at the current logits with z_old unchanged, and take one step z -= lr * dz. A group whose rewards all tie still takes the step (only the KL term acts) and counts in zero_groups.
