Each task of a loop gets an automation level from 0 to 5 from three questions: was AI used, what share of the work did it do, and what is the human's role (none, approves, directs or drives). The loop is then summarised by its mean and its weakest link, the lowest-rated task, where a person still sits beside it.

task_level asks who steers. A human who drives does the work with AI help (level 1 or 2), one who directs sets the course while AI does large chunks (2 or 3), and one who approves only signs off (3 or 4). The AI's share of the work picks the lower or higher level, and a share exactly on the threshold takes the higher. automation_level(tasks) returns (levels, mean, weakest), and a tie for weakest goes to the name that sorts first.
