A loop that keeps the best of many noisy scores shows a rising line even if every proposal is noise, because the maximum of many noisy numbers rises. A real trend is several accepted gains that each clear the noise floor (the score change that re-running the same agent produces by itself), with none of them carrying the result.

In real_gains(run), an accepted step's gain is its incumbent (the best kept score so far) minus the previous step's incumbent. It counts only if strictly above run.noise_floor; rejected steps change nothing, and the first step has nothing to compare with. sustained_trend(run) returns a Verdict. It fails with "real accept" in the reason when there are fewer than MIN_ACCEPTS real gains, and with "lucky" when the largest gain exceeds MAX_SHARE of the total.
