A budget is fixed only if it is enforced, so the check adds up costs and names the first step where the running total crosses the limit: "over budget" invites an argument, "exceeded at step 27" does not. The four verdicts are then shown side by side and never averaged, because failing any one means Level 1 (all four conditions met) has not been reached.

budget_ok(run) keeps a running total of cost_usd and fails at the first step whose total passes budget_usd; spending exactly the budget is allowed. Then len(steps) must not pass max_attempts. report_card(improver, baseline) returns a dictionary keyed by CONDITIONS in order: condition 1 compares the two runs, conditions 2 to 4 judge the improver. render(card) writes one line per condition (PASS or FAIL, the name, the reason), then a last line, indented like the rest, that reads Level 1: met or Level 1: NOT met.

budget $20, costs 5, 5, 5, 4, 3: totals 5, 10, 15, 19, 22
-> fails: budget $20 exceeded at step 5
