Measuring Recursive Self-Improvement

When a system rewrites the agent that does its research, the hard question is not whether the numbers went up but whether anything improved. This course is the measurement behind Artifact 4: Weco's four Level-1 conditions as tests you can run, taxonomies of how much AI research is automated and what the published estimates say, the economics of a loop, an audit checklist for claims of self-improvement, and a reading pack for what comes next. Its lab is the checker you point at your own outer loop.

How to use this course

🧪 Four tests

A fair baseline, a sustained trend, generalisation to unseen tasks and a fixed budget: each is a check you can run on a log.

📏 Self-reported, dated

Every published figure about automation or self-improvement is marked as the authors' own and dated, so you can see what has been checked by anyone else.

🔧 For Artifact 4

The lab writes the judge for your outer loop, and the workbench measures what an improver's next dollar buys.

Chapters are tagged Deep dive, Core or Quick intro so you know where to spend effort. The lab uses Python's standard library and fixture logs, so python3 check.py runs offline, for free, and gives the same answer every time. The workbench runs on the lab VM and spends under a dollar of API calls.
