Twenty tasks is a small sample, so "the new design scored higher" needs an error bar. The comparison is paired, task by task, because the trials of one task are not independent evidence, and two methods, the bootstrap and the sign test, ask whether the differences are more than noise.

paired_bootstrap_ci builds many new lists of the same length by drawing from the differences at random, repeats allowed, takes the mean of each, and reports the middle 95% of those means as an interval. Follow the stated procedure exactly, the same draws in the same order, so results are reproducible. sign_test_p ignores sizes and ties: with no real difference, wins and losses behave like fair coin flips, so it adds the exact chance of a split at least as lopsided, doubled for both sides. decide applies the docstring's rule: success decides if its interval excludes zero, otherwise cost does, otherwise "none".

8 wins, 2 losses: at most 2 losses in 10 fair flips = 56/1024
doubled for both sides: p = 0.109, so not yet convincing
