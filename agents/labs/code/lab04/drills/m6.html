Patterns compose, and budgets keep the composition honest. The support desk classifies each ticket, sends tracking questions through the gated chain and the rest to a specialist, gives every ticket its own budget, and hands to a human anything that fails a router check, a gate or its budget.

process_ticket follows the order in its docstring and records calls, tokens, dollars and seconds from the ticket's own BudgetedMeter. Any BudgetExceeded ends in a human ticket with the reason budget: calls (or tokens, usd), like any other escalation. percentile uses the nearest-rank rule, sort then take the value at 1-based rank ceil(q / 100 * n), and raises ValueError for an empty list or a q outside (0, 100]. run_desk shares one model, makes a fresh budget per ticket and summarises. Look at the spread, not only the mean: a budget is a promise about the worst case.

percentile of 1..10:  q = 50 -> rank 5 -> 5
                      q = 95 -> rank ceil(9.5) = 10 -> 10
