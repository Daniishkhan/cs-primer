An attack written once, without trying to beat a particular defence, understates the danger. An adaptive attacker knows the defence and tries rewrites until one gets through, and a defence that falls to a weak one of these will fall to a stronger one.

adaptive_asr(defence, attacks, mutations) takes each attack through the chains from mutation_chains in order (as written first, then single rewrites, then pairs), applying a chain left to right to the attack's text, and stops at the first chain for which attack_succeeded holds. It returns the share of attacks that got through and a dict from attack id to the winning chain, its rewrite names joined with + or "as written". scoreboard evaluates each defence and adds its adaptive rate. choose(rows, min_utility) applies the utility floor first, then takes the lowest adaptive rate, then the higher utility, then the earlier name, or returns None.

rows (adaptive, utility): A (0.0, 0.9)  B (0.0, 1.0)  C (0.25, 1.0)
choose(rows, 0.95) -> B: A is under the floor, then B beats C
