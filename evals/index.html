Evals, Guardrails & Safe Operation

Whether an AI system works is an empirical question, and most teams answer it by trying a few examples and feeling good. This course teaches the alternative: error analysis on real traces, assertions and harnesses, LLM judges calibrated against people, error bars, domain benchmarks that stay honest, agent evaluation, hallucination and how to reduce it, prompt injection and the defences that hold, guardrails, red teaming, observability and the governance frameworks that turn all of it into evidence.

How to use this course

🔬 Look at the data

Every method starts from reading traces and naming failures. Metrics come after, and they come with error bars.

⚖️ Judges are models too

LLM judges are measured against human labels before anyone trusts their numbers.

🧯 Assume an attacker

Injection defences are tested against adaptive attackers, and the ones that hold are architectural.

Chapters are tagged Deep dive, Core or Quick intro so you know where to spend effort. Labs use Python's standard library and scripted stand-in models, so python3 check.py runs offline, for free, and gives the same answer every time; each lab page shows how to point it at a real model when you want to.
