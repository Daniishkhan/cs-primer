A classifier scores how much a text looks like an injection, but a score is not a decision: a low threshold blocks more attacks and more harmless requests, a high one fewer of both. The principled choice is to fix how many false alarms you can tolerate on benign data and take the lowest threshold that stays within it.

With n benign scores you may flag k = floor(max_fpr × n) of them. Sort the scores from highest to lowest and take the (k + 1)-th as the threshold. A text is flagged only when its score is strictly above it, so ties at the cut are not flagged. ClassifierGuard.screen returns (score > threshold, score). The constructor (given) calls your pick_threshold on a calibration set of benign texts.
