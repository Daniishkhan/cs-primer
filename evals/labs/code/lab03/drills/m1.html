A benchmark file is long and every row looks plausible, so a validator reads it for you and lists the defects: a missing gold answer, a bad label, a repeated id, a repeated question. To tell whether a question repeats, cut each question into shingles, the runs of k consecutive words, and compare the two sets with Jaccard similarity: the size of the overlap divided by the size of the union.

shingles builds on the given words; a text shorter than k words has none, and jaccard of two empty sets is 0.0, not an error. validate compares each question with every earlier item and reports at most one near-duplicate, for the best match, with an inclusive threshold. If both golds exist and differ after norm, the pair is worse than a copy: no system can be right on both. A flag is a lead for a person, not a verdict.

"what is the fee now"  -> adds {the fee now}: Jaccard 2/3
