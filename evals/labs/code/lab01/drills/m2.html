A runner calls the system under test and records one of three outcomes per attempt: pass, fail (the reply broke a check) or error (the call itself raised, such as a timeout). An error is a fact about the run, not the model, so record it and carry on, but still count it against the score.

run_suite asks every case k times, because a model sampled at a non-zero temperature can pass once and fail next time. Use grade on each reply and keep one reasons entry per attempt. A case's score is the share of its attempts that passed, with errors left in the denominator (the given Result.score). summarise reports the mean of the per-case scores, the error and attempt counts, and a score per tag with its case count beside it, since a slice of eight cases is noisy.

Three attempts that go fail, error, pass score 1/3 and add one to the error count.
