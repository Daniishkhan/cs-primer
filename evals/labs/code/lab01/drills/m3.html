A score from 160 questions would change on a different 160, because the questions are only a sample. A standard error is the typical size of that change, and an error bar is about two of them either side of the score.

  • mean_se: the sample standard deviation (divide by n−1) over √n; one value gives 0.0.
  • wilson: for small n or rates near 0% or 100%, where the textbook interval calls 20 of 20 certain. It keeps every true rate p that the observed rate is within 1.96 standard errors of, measured at p itself; solving that is a quadratic.
  • clustered_se: paraphrases of a question are answered alike, so 160 cases may be worth 40. One item per cluster must give mean_se.
  • bootstrap_ci: redraw the eval B times with replacement and read the middle 95% of the means. Draw whole groups and pool their values (not their means), or the interval is far too narrow.
wilson(20, 20) = 0.839 to 1.0
clustered_se([1,1,0,0], ["a","a","b","b"]) = 0.382
