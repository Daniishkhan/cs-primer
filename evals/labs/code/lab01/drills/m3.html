A score from 160 questions would change on a different 160, because the questions are only a sample. A standard error is the typical size of that change, and an error bar is about two of them either side of the score.

  • mean_se: the standard error is the sample standard deviation over √n; one value gives 0.0.
  • wilson: for small n or extreme rates, where the textbook interval calls 20 of 20 certain. It keeps every candidate rate p within 1.96 standard errors of the observed rate, measured at p itself; solving that is a quadratic.
  • clustered_se: related cases, such as paraphrases of one question, form a cluster and are answered alike, so 160 cases may be worth 40. One item per cluster must give mean_se.
  • bootstrap_ci: redraw the eval B times with replacement and read the middle 95% of the means. Draw whole groups, pooling their values, or the interval is far too narrow.
wilson(20, 20) -> 0.839 to 1.0
1 1 0 0 in groups a a b b -> clustered SE 0.382
