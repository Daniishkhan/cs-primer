An agent that succeeds 90% of the time at ten dollars a task and one that succeeds 80% at ten cents cannot be compared on accuracy alone, so every trial gets a price. Agents spend tokens at every step and the conversation they send grows, so cost per task varies far more than cost per model call.

trial_cost turns a record's tokens into dollars; the prices are per million tokens. cost_report covers every trial, failures included, because failures cost money too: cost per success is total spend divided by successes (None if there are none), so it is higher than cost per attempt. The mean hides runaway loops, so report the tail. The 95th percentile is by nearest rank: sort the costs and take position ceil(0.95 × N), counting from 1, so the 19th of 20 and not the maximum. Also report the maximum and how many trials hit the step cap. An empty list raises ValueError.
