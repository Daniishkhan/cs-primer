Verbosity bias is a judge preferring longer answers even when the extra words say nothing. It points the same way in both orders, so swapping cannot expose it; instead, append filler to an answer and judge it again, since a judge that read only the content would not change its verdict.

pad_answer appends the six-word PAD_SENTENCE after a space, repeated enough times to add at least extra_words words; zero or fewer returns the answer unchanged. padding_probe judges every item before and after padding. Among the items the judge failed, report the share that flip to PASS; among those it passed, the share that flip to FAIL. No human labels are needed, because the judge is compared with itself, so the probe can run on production traffic. A share over zero items is None.

pad_answer("Yes.", 7)
  -> "Yes. Thank you for getting in touch. Thank you for getting in touch."
