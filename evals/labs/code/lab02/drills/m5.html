A judge's pass rate has its mistakes mixed in, measured by its true-positive rate (TPR: the share of good answers it passes) and true-negative rate (TNR: the share of bad answers it fails). If p is the true pass rate, the judge reports p × TPR + (1 − p) × (1 − TNR), so you can solve backwards for p.

rogan_gladen is that equation solved for p and clipped to 0 to 1; if TPR + TNR − 1 is not positive, the judge is no better than a coin flip. Unlike accuracy, the rates do not depend on how many answers are good, so they transfer from a failure-enriched calibration set to production. correct_pass_rate measures them on the calibration pairs and corrects the production pass rate. corrected_ci redraws both data sets with replacement in every repetition (use _tpr_tnr) and skips repetitions that cannot be corrected.

TPR 0.9, TNR 0.7, observed 0.6: (0.6 + 0.7 − 1) / (0.9 + 0.7 − 1) = 0.5.
