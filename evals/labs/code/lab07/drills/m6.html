A release gate blocks a change when the suite shows it got worse, by a rule fixed before the results. Two rules catch different things: an interval on the average change guards against broad decline, and a rule about individual critical cases guards against a local break that an average cannot see.

For each case, d = (candidate passes − baseline passes) / k. paired_ci is a percentile bootstrap over those differences: resample the cases, not the trials, since trials of one case share its difficulty. A hard regression is a case that passed k of k with the baseline and at most max_after of k with the candidate. gate reports each slice, then ALL, with the mean difference and interval. It ships only if the lower bound of ALL's interval is at least −tolerance and no hard regression sits in a critical slice.

In 120 cases, one critical case drops from 3 of 3 to 1 of 3: the mean moves by only −0.006, but the hard-regression rule catches it.
