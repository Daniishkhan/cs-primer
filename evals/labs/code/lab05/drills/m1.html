Two checks on a citation need no model: does the cited document exist, and do the quoted words occur in it? People copy loosely (case, line breaks, curly quotes), so both sides are compared in a normalised form, yet the span you report must point into the original text.

normalise_with_map returns (norm, idx), where idx[i] is the original offset of norm[i]; a collapsed run of whitespace points at its first character, and nothing is stripped. verify_quote splits the quote at an ellipsis into fragments and finds them in order, each search starting where the last one ended, so they cannot overlap or go backwards. It rejects a quote that is too short in total, because two or three words match some document by chance. Convert the span back through idx: the end is idx[b - 1] + 1. check_citation checks that the document exists before it looks for the quote.

normalise_with_map("Hi  There")
  -> ("hi there", [0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8])
