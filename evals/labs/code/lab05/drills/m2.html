A quotation can be real and still not back the claim, so the question that matters is entailment: if the cited text is true, must the claim be true? A judge answers SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED or, when the text does not address the claim at all, NOT_ENOUGH_INFO.

judge_claim is a few rules you can read, and its score is the best overlap (the share of the claim's content words that one sentence contains), whatever the label. The overlap bar, checked first, asks whether any sentence addresses the claim. A negation on one side only means the text says the opposite. A number in the claim that the sentence lacks means a different figure. evaluate_judge returns all nine (gold, predicted) cells, zeros included, and the precision of SUPPORTED, which matters most: a false support stamps a wrong claim as checked.

Against "Marlow Pool has capacity for 300 visitors", the claim "Marlow Pool can hold 300 visitors" is SUPPORTED with score 1.0, and with 250 it is CONTRADICTED.
