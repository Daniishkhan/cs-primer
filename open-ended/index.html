Search, Evolution & Self-Improving Agents

Some of the most interesting AI systems do not answer a question; they keep searching. This course covers the ideas behind them: tree search and bandits, evolutionary algorithms, novelty search and quality-diversity, open-ended algorithms, language models as mutation operators in program evolution, agents that design agents and improve their own code, automated science, and the integrity problem that comes with all of it: a search loop will find every loophole in its evaluator.

How to use this course

🌱 Stepping stones

Objectives can deceive. Novelty and diversity often find better solutions than chasing the score directly.

🧬 Evolution with LLMs

You build a small FunSearch-style loop and a meta-agent search that runs offline.

🔒 Integrity first

Held-out evaluation and hardened evaluators are taught as the price of admission for self-improving systems.

Chapters are tagged Deep dive, Core or Quick intro so you know where to spend effort. Labs use Python's standard library and scripted stand-in models, so python3 check.py runs offline, for free, and gives the same answer every time; each lab page shows how to point it at a real model when you want to.
