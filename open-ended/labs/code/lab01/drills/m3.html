One way to explore is to keep a belief about each arm's win rate, draw one random sample from each belief, and pull the arm whose sample is largest (Thompson sampling). An arm you know little about has a wide belief, so it sometimes draws high and gets pulled. As evidence arrives the belief narrows and the exploring fades by itself, with no constant to tune.

The belief for an arm with w wins and l losses is a Beta distribution: a curve over win rates from 0 to 1. The 1 + in the parameters makes it flat when nothing is known. Wins are totals[i]; losses are the pulls that did not win. rng.betavariate(a, b) draws one sample, and the draw order matters, so go through the arms in index order. Return the arm with the largest draw. Untried arms need no special case.

0 wins, 0 losses: flat, mean 0.500, standard deviation 0.289
70 wins, 30 losses: mean 0.696, standard deviation 0.045
