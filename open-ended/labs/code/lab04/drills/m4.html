Models are good at different jobs: one starts strong approaches, another repairs them well. Choosing which to call is one more bandit decision, handled by widening the arms: one arm per pair of action kind and model, each with its own list of gains and its own draw from the given thompson_draw. The largest draw names both what to do and who does it.

self.arms lists ("wider", 0), ("deeper", 0), ("wider", 1), ... and self.hist maps each arm to its gains. With no answers yet nothing can be refined, so draw for the wider arms only. Otherwise draw once for every arm, in arm order, and take the largest, the earlier arm on a tie. A deeper arm refines env.best.id, or uses pick_parent if self.lam is set. observe files the gain under (kind, model): the model is the last item of either action shape. model_shares counts one kind's steps by model and divides by their total.
