Always refining the best answer can get stuck. An answer embodies an approach, and repairs only polish its details until the approach itself is the limit, so the answer that leads now may be flat from here on. A better rule samples the next parent (the answer to refine) from the good answers, and gives less weight to answers that have already had many children.

parent_weights(scores, kids, lam) gives every answer a weight, then divides by the total so the weights sum to 1. The sigmoid (a smooth step from 0 to 1, already written as sigmoid) rises with score, so weak answers keep a small chance. The divisor 1 + kids penalises answers that already have children. The pressure lam sets how sharp the step is: large means refine the best, small means refine anything. pick_parent makes one rng.choices draw over list positions and returns the chosen node's .id, which is not its position in the list.
