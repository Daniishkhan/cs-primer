A two-layer network is two matrix products with a bend (ReLU) between them. Its weights must start random (identical hidden units would get identical gradients and stay identical) and at the right size: a unit adds up one product per input, so more inputs need smaller weights to keep the signal steady.

init_params draws every weight with gauss(0, sigma) from one random.Random(seed) and leaves the biases at zero; the two sigmas are in the docstring (the factor 2 for the ReLU layer makes up for ReLU zeroing about half its inputs). forward follows the three lines of its docstring and also returns a cache of every intermediate value. Training needs them again in the backward pass, which is why it uses more memory than prediction. Do not modify params.
