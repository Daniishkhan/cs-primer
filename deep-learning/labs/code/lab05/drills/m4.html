A language model's scores become probabilities, and you can reshape them before drawing a token: a temperature sharpens or flattens them, and truncation cuts the unlikely tail so that one nonsense token cannot derail what follows. After any cut, the survivors must be rescaled to sum to 1.

softmax turns scores into probabilities: it divides the logits by temperature first (below 1 sharpens, above 1 flattens), subtracts the maximum, and raises ValueError for 0 or less. Top-k keeps a fixed number of tokens. Top-p keeps the fewest top tokens whose mass reaches p, including the token that crosses it. Min-p keeps tokens at least frac times as likely as the best.
