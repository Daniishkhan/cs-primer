A word's meaning shows in the company it keeps: words that turn up among the same neighbours mean similar things. So the training data for word vectors is pairs, a centre word and a context word that sits within a few positions of it (the window), cut straight from raw text. No neural network is involved yet.

Words become ids, their positions in the vocabulary list, because later an id will pick a row of a table; sorting by count, ties alphabetical, makes every id identical on every machine. make_pairs must drop words missing from index before it looks at windows, and must never pair across sentences. To check your pair count, remember that each position contributes one pair per neighbour inside the window and the sentence.
