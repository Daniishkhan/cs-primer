Word vectors are compared by direction, not length. The cosine similarity of two vectors is their dot product divided by both lengths: 1 for the same direction, 0 for perpendicular, -1 for opposite. Relations show up as consistent offsets, so "a is to b as c is to ?" is answered by the word nearest to b - a + c.

nearest ranks every other word by cosine with the query word. In analogy the target is often closest to b or c themselves, because the offset is small next to the vectors. With exclude on, drop a, b and c from the candidates; without it, most answers are one of the question words, which the checker confirms. analogy_accuracy counts how often the top answer is d.

man:king :: woman:?   target = king - man + woman
inputs allowed: woman, queen, princess;  inputs excluded: queen, princess, girl
