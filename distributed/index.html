Distributed Systems, Deep

Networking Part 6 gave you the shape of the problem: partial failure, unreliable clocks, replication, quorums and a toy Raft. This course goes underneath. We'll make the models precise, meet the results that say what is impossible, and then build what is possible anyway: failure detectors, leases and fencing, Paxos and Raft in depth, atomic commit, CRDTs and exactly-once streams. Along the way you'll learn how the people who build these systems convince themselves they are right: history checkers, deterministic simulation and TLA+.

How to use this course

🔗 Deeper, not again

This course assumes failure and timeouts, clocks, replication and Raft basics, plus isolation levels. Each chapter links back and then goes further.

🎯 Depth is labelled

Deep dive chapters (system models, failure detection, leases, consistency models, Paxos, Raft, atomic commit, CRDTs, exactly-once, testing) are what senior engineers argue about in design reviews. Quick intro chapters map the rest.

🧪 Simulate, then break it

Every lab runs a whole cluster inside one deterministic Python process, so you can drop, delay and reorder messages on purpose and replay any failure from its seed. Optional cloud-VM extensions take the ideas across real regions.
