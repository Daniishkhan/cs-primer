With no real difference between two versions, one run of each is a coin toss: either can come out lower. A paired test removes most of that luck by running both on the same seeds, taking the difference per seed, and keeping the change only if the average difference is clearly bigger than the wobble among those differences.

paired_test(diffs) turns the per-seed differences into t = mean / (sd / sqrt(n)), the average gain counted in units of its own uncertainty (sd is the sample standard deviation). It also returns df, the degrees of freedom (n - 1), and whether |t| beats t_critical(df) (given). keep_or_revert evaluates both configs on every seed and forms best.proxy - cand.proxy; scores are errors, so positive means the candidate is better. Keep only if the mean is positive and significant, because the test also flags significant losses.

diffs 0.2, -0.1, 0.3, 0.0, 0.1: mean 0.1, sd 0.158, t = 1.41
