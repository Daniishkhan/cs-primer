One experiment's gap between the two scores is mostly noise, but a trend is not: add up the gains of the kept experiments as they happen and watch whether the proxy total keeps climbing while the held-out total has stopped. That pattern is a loop overfitting its own yardstick.

cumulative(history) returns three lists with one entry per experiment, so a reverted experiment repeats the previous total. ols_slope(ys) is the least-squares slope of ys against 0, 1, 2, ...: the sum of (x - mean x)(y - mean y) divided by the sum of (x - mean x) squared. detect_overfit slides a window along the totals and fires only when both hold: the gap is rising and the held-out total is not. Both are needed: a short run can gain more than a long one from a real change, so a widening gap alone proves nothing. text_chart follows its docstring exactly.
