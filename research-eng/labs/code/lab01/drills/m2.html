Run the same setup twice with different random seeds and the scores differ, though nothing was changed. How large that difference gets by chance alone is the noise floor, and a gain smaller than it has shown nothing.

mean is the average. sample_sd is the standard deviation (the typical distance of a score from the mean), dividing by n - 1 because the mean was estimated from the same scores. noise_floor(config, seeds, store) sends every seed through evaluate, so repeats come from the store, then reports mean, sd and floor for both scores, proxy and held-out. The floor is 1.96 * sqrt(2) * sd. The sqrt(2) is there because the difference of two noisy runs is noisier than one run (the variances add), and 1.96 covers 95% of such differences.
