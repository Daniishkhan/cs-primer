## Course brief
A10 serves **Artifact 2 (mini-FML search study)**, an AIDE-style tree-search agent compared head to head with greedy hill-climbing on three FML-Bench tasks, and **Artifact 3 (evaluator-hardening kit)**, a KernelBench subset where the reader first breaks the grader, then hardens it and measures the hacking rate. Chapters: harness engineering as research (the ladder from context engineering to workflows to self-improving harnesses; place ACE (Agentic Context Engineering), Meta-Harness, STOP and the Darwin Gödel Machine on it), AIDE's tree search over experiments (draft/debug/improve nodes, selection, the FML-Bench finding that greedy is about as good as tree search, opportunity density), public and private splits with typed and attested results and hacking rate as a first-class metric, kernel engineering for agents (Triton first, CUDA at reading level; KernelBench and its verified variants with hidden multi-distribution tests; robust-kbench; end-to-end timing), and the benchmarks research agents are judged on (MLE-bench and its Lite subset, PaperBench, RE-Bench, FML-Bench, KernelBench). A6's chapters on LLM judges (evl 03), eval statistics (evl 04), building benchmarks (evl 06) and contamination (evl 07) stay in A6: link to them, never repeat them. Sources to fetch: AIDE (arXiv 2502.13138) and github.com/WecoAI/aideml; FML-Bench (github.com/qrzou/FML-bench and its arXiv paper; the roadmap cites a v2 as arXiv 2605.17373: verify); AIDE² (roadmap cites arXiv 2609.26457: verify) and Weco's blog; Lilian Weng's post on harness engineering (the roadmap links a mirror at lilithai.dev; find the original on lilianweng.github.io); ACE (Agentic Context Engineering, arXiv 2510.04618); Meta-Harness (find it); STOP (arXiv 2310.02304); KernelBench (arXiv 2502.10517) and any "KernelBench-Verified"; robust-kbench (arXiv 2509.14279); SpecBench (roadmap: arXiv 2605.21384: verify); MLE-bench (arXiv 2410.07095); PaperBench (arXiv 2504.01848); RE-Bench (arXiv 2411.15114).
