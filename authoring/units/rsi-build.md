# Unit: rsi-build

Course: **A12 Measuring Recursive Self-Improvement** (id `rsi`, folder `rsi-measurement/`), a new course serving Artifact 4 (AIDE²-in-miniature).

## Course brief
A12 is the measurement behind **Artifact 4**: an outer agent rewrites the reader's Artifact 2 agent on a six-task mix scored on private splits under a fixed dollar budget, judged by Weco's four Level-1 conditions (a fair baseline given the same budget and attempts; a sustained trend over several steps, not one lucky accept; generalisation to tasks never seen during search; a fixed physical budget stated up front), then an ignition test (swap the discovered agent into the outer loop and report whether it is a better improver). Chapters: what counts as self-improvement (the four conditions as tests you can run; the standards every artifact meets), automation levels for AI research (Epoch's AL0 to AL5 taxonomy, Anthropic's measurement method and its reported share of work at a high level, OpenAI's stated research-intern milestone; every figure marked as self-reported and dated), the economics of a loop (cost per accepted improvement, diminishing versus compounding returns, Burtsev's R_AI ratio, loop elasticities from the economics-of-RSI paper), reading self-improvement claims (an audit checklist applied to DGM, AlphaEvolve, the AI Scientist, AIDE²; pairs with A7 studio S2), and a reading pack pointing at dl:23, dl:24, oe:22 and the 2026 successors of the Darwin Gödel Machine (Huxley-Gödel Machine, HyperAgents, Red Queen Gödel Machine). It builds on A7 chapters 12, 13, 14, 16, 21 and A6 chapter 04. Sources to fetch (the roadmap's links; verify every one): Weco's blog post on first evidence of recursive self-improvement and the AIDE² paper (roadmap: arXiv 2609.26457), Epoch's "Toward an O*NET for AI R&D", Anthropic Institute "Measuring the pace of AI development", the Economics of RSI paper (roadmap: arXiv 2609.15802), Burtsev's R_AI (roadmap: arXiv 2609.00137), the Huxley-Gödel Machine (find it), HyperAgents (arXiv 2603.19461), the Red Queen Gödel Machine (find it), OpenAI's statement of an automated research-intern goal (find the primary source).

5. Do not edit `assets/js/catalog.js`, shared JS/CSS, or files outside your assignment. Do not run git commands that change state. Remove any `__pycache__` you create.
