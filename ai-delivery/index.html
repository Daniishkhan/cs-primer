Applied AI Delivery Studio

Applied AI engineers sit between a customer's messy problem and a working system. The job is less about novel algorithms than about finding the real problem, testing the riskiest assumption quickly, building an evaluation the customer trusts, and getting a pilot into production inside someone else's security rules. This capstone teaches that work through chapters on discovery, delivery and domains, and eight take-home studios modelled on how applied AI teams hire.

How to use this course

🗣️ Find the real problem

Discovery interviews and expert-knowledge capture come before any model choice.

📦 Ship inside constraints

On-premises deployment, restricted model menus, security reviews and cost models are part of the design.

📝 Take-home practice

Eight timed briefs with deliverables, worked answers and rubrics, like the assignments applied AI teams set.

Chapters are tagged Deep dive, Core or Quick intro so you know where to spend effort. Labs use Python's standard library and scripted stand-in models, so python3 check.py runs offline, for free, and gives the same answer every time; each lab page shows how to point it at a real model when you want to.
