The 95% in a 95% interval describes the method, not one interval. On many fresh samples from a world where you know the truth, about 95% of the intervals should contain it. That fraction is the coverage, and measuring it audits the interval itself.

  • coverage(interval_fn, sampler, truth, E, seed) makes one random.Random(seed) and passes it to sampler every time. A fresh generator per experiment would repeat one sample E times.
  • ab_report assembles the toolkit for a two-arm test. Its interval for the lift uses the unpooled standard error, because the arms are separate samples. Its z and p come from two_proportion_z_test, which pools them. Both are right, for different questions.
  • The verdict needs p below alpha and a lift of the right sign. n_needed comes from sample_size.
