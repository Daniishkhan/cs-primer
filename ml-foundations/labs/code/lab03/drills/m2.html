On the valley, the steep coordinate's gradient flips sign every step while the gentle one keeps its sign. Keep a running sum of recent gradients and the alternating parts cancel while the consistent part builds up. That is momentum: a velocity v that adds each new gradient to a decayed copy of itself, and the weights step along v.

  • The decay β (0.9 by default) gives the velocity a memory of about 1/(1 − β) = 10 steps.
  • The velocity is state. Create self.v as zeros on the first call, when the number of weights is known, and keep it on the instance so two optimisers never share one.
  • Return a fresh list, without modifying w.
constant gradient 1, lr 0.01, beta 0.9
v = 1, 1.9, 2.71, ...  moves 0.01, 0.019, 0.0271, ...
the step settles at lr / (1 - beta) = 0.1
