A matrix is a table of numbers (here a list of rows) and also a function. An (m, n) matrix takes a length-n vector to a length-m vector whose entry i is the dot product of row i with the input. Multiplying two matrices does that for every column of the second one at once.

  • For A of shape (m, n) and B of shape (n, p), entry (i, j) of AB is row i of A dotted with column j of B, so AB has shape (m, p). Your lists hold rows, so transpose B to reach its columns.
  • The inner sizes must agree. If they do not, raise ValueError with a message that shows both shapes, as shape (given) reports them.
  • Neither transpose nor matmul may change its arguments.
A = [[1, 2], [3, 4]], B = [[5, 6], [7, 8]]
entry (0, 1) = (1, 2) . (6, 8) = 22;  AB = [[19, 22], [43, 50]]
