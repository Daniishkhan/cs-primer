To find more than one eigenpair (an eigenvector and its eigenvalue), remove the first from the matrix. For a symmetric matrix with eigenvalue λ and unit eigenvector v, subtracting λ v vT keeps the eigenvectors but sets v's eigenvalue to 0. That is deflation. PCA applies it to the covariance matrix of the centred data (how each pair of features varies together). The eigenvectors are the directions of greatest variance, and each eigenvalue is the variance along its direction.

  • top_k_eigen works on a copy, so the caller's matrix is unchanged. Each pass records the pair, then deflates with the given outer.
  • reconstruct centres each row, takes its coefficient along every component, and rebuilds the row from the mean. After keeping k components, the squared error left equals the sum of the eigenvalues you dropped.
