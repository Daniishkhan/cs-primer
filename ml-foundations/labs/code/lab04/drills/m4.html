The softmax turns raw scores (logits) into probabilities, but computed literally it breaks. An exponential of 1000 overflows, and a probability like e−800 underflows to 0, so its loss −ln p becomes infinite. The fix: softmax is unchanged when the same constant is added to every logit, so subtract the largest and the biggest exponent is e0 = 1.

  • logsumexp is m + ln ∑ exp(z − m) with m the maximum. An empty list raises ValueError. Entries of −inf are legal masks (their exponential is 0), and a list of all −inf gives −inf, not nan from (−inf) − (−inf).
  • Work in log space: ln pi = zi − logsumexp(z), and never form a probability on the way. softmax exponentiates that result, and cross_entropy is minus the log-softmax at the target.
  • Do not modify the list you are given.
logits (1000, 1001, 1002): softmax (0.0900, 0.2447, 0.6652)
logits (0, 200), true class 0: loss exactly 200, not infinity
