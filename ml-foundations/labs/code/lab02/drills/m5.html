A classifier ends with one raw score, a logit, per class. Softmax turns the logits into probabilities that sum to 1. Cross-entropy is minus the log of the probability given to the true class: small when the model is confident and right, large when it is confidently wrong.

  • With logits near 1000, exp overflows. Subtract the largest logit m first: the log of the sum of exp(z) equals m plus the log of the sum of exp(z - m). That is logsumexp. Use m as a plain number, not a Value: it is a constant shift, so it adds no gradient path.
  • softmax and cross_entropy then follow from logsumexp alone. A target outside the classes raises IndexError.
  • Your engine should produce the gradient with no extra rule: the probabilities minus the one-hot target (1 for the true class, 0 elsewhere), which sums to zero.
