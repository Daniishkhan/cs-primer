Evolution here is a loop: keep a few good programs, mutate parents picked at random but favouring the better ones, score the children and keep the best. Elitism, copying the best programs unchanged into the next generation, means the best score can never get worse.

Build on the docstring's steps with four rules. Score children on the training instances only: the held-out instances, which the search never sees, are for judging the winner honestly. Archive.add returns a bool, so fetch the new Entry from archive.entries by its canonical_hash, and pass the parent's hash so the log is a family tree. Duplicates are skipped, so early populations can be shorter than pop_size. Draw every random number from the one random.Random(seed) and break ties as tournament does, so a rerun gives the same log.
