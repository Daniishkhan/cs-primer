Evolution here is a loop: keep a few good programs, mutate parents picked at random but favouring the better ones, score the children and keep the best. Elitism, copying the best programs unchanged into the next generation, means the best score can never get worse.

  • tournament (given) picks a few programs at random and returns the best. Good programs usually win, but middling ones sometimes do, which keeps the population varied.
  • Score children on the training instances only. The held-out instances, which the search never sees, are for judging the winner honestly.
  • Archive.add returns a bool, so fetch the new Entry from archive.entries by its canonical_hash. Pass the parent's hash, so every parent is in the archive.
  • Skipped duplicates make early populations shorter than pop_size. Take every random number from the one random.Random(seed), and sort with the tie-break of Archive.best, so a rerun gives the same log.
